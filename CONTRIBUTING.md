# Contribution Guidelines

## Canonical terminology

Contributions must preserve the canonical meanings of S, D, and E. A proposal that changes a core definition must be submitted as an explicit theoretical amendment rather than silently modifying terminology.

## Contribution types

- correction of wording or references;
- worked case study;
- formal or computational model;
- empirical test;
- critical comparison with another theory;
- translation;
- visualization or teaching material.

## Minimum requirements

Every substantive contribution should state:

1. the S, D, and E of the analyzed system;
2. the selected generative path or interacting paths;
3. the evidence supporting each mapping;
4. the limits of the claim;
5. whether the contribution interprets, extends, tests, or criticizes SDE.

## Authorship
