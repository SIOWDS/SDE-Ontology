# SDE Ontology｜SDE 本体论

> **In E, through D, S emerges.｜在 E 中，经 D，成 S。**

SDE Ontology is an original generative ontology proposed by **Dr. Wang Desheng (王德生博士)**. It studies how an apparent structure emerges, changes, and reorganizes through the coupled operation of:

- **S — Structural Disclosure State｜结构显露态**
- **D — Difference Sequence｜差异序列**
- **E — Entangled Feature System｜特征纠缠系统**

SDE does not treat S, D, and E as three isolated substances. They are three mutually defining dimensions of one generative process.

## Core equations｜核心三方程

\[
S=F(D,E),\qquad D=G(S,E),\qquad E=H(S,D)
\]

The first equation explains emergence, the second explains path formation, and the third explains environmental or relational rewriting. Together they reject one-way causation and describe a recursively coupled system.

## Six generative paths｜六路径

The six paths are the six ordered traversals of S, D, and E:

1. S → D → E
2. S → E → D
3. D → S → E
4. D → E → S
5. E → S → D
6. E → D → S

They are not merely permutations of letters. Each path represents a different starting constraint, mediating mechanism, and mode of emergence.

## SDE and SIO｜SDE 与 SIO

SIO is not a parallel ontology independent of SDE. In this framework, **SIO is a mature and relatively stable disclosure within the S dimension**:

- Subjective Body｜主体
- Interactive Body｜互动体
- Objective Body｜客体

Accordingly, SIO is best understood as one stabilized perspective on an SDE process, not as the ultimate ontological foundation.
